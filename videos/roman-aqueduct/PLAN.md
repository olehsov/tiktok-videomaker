# PLAN — Roman Aqueduct: A River With No Pump (`roman-aqueduct`)

## 1. Summary
- **Concept:** one long low-poly side-view cut-away of an aqueduct, with the spring on the left and the town on the right. A camera group moves over the profile. The diorama gains one element per step: tiny slope, tunnel, covered channel, lining, arches, siphon, settling tank, hatches, splitter tank. A pigeon observer goes from the dry fountain, to the arch top, to drinking at the flowing fountain.
- **Duration:** `en` = 116.151 s (voice 114.651 s + 1.5 s tail, `timings.en.json`, source `elevenlabs`).
- **Languages:** `en` only. All geometry and timing are language-agnostic: times come only from cues and beat starts, and no English word literals are used in `src/`.

## 2. Composition

### 2.1 Pipeline
`node build.mjs en` reads `script.en.json`, `timings.en.json` and `src/scenes.mjs`, then writes `build/en/index.html` plus `assets/voice.mp3`. The output is one root composition (`data-composition-id="main"`, 1080×1920, 116.151 s) with one paused GSAP timeline, `window.__timelines["main"]`. Overlays (plates, callouts, labels, gauge, meter) and karaoke captions are generated by `build.mjs` from `onScreen`. Scenes never draw text from the script except the recap step numbers (§3, S12).

### 2.2 Tracks / layers (`data-track-index` is the Studio lane; paint order is z-index)
| Layer | Element | track | z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Music (only if `audio/music.loop.en.mp3` exists; none now) | `<audio id="music">` | -1 | – |
| Voice | `<audio id="voice">` | 0 | – |
| Scenes S1–S13 (full-frame clips, one per beat group) | `#sc-<id>` | 1 | 1 |

Each scene's stage SVG is built in this order (later = in front):
1. **Fixed back** (screen coordinates, not moved by the camera): `sky({horizonY:1300})`, sun.
2. **Camera group** `<g id="{I}-cam" transform="translate(tx ty) scale(s)">`, which holds the whole world (§3.0 layer order).
3. **Fixed front** (screen coordinates): clouds, rain, recap markers.

### 2.3 Files the builder creates or changes
- `src/world.mjs`: world constants, `L(x)`, geometry tables, camera framings, `camStr()`, `toScreen()` and `drawWorld(I, st, slots)`.
- `src/effects.mjs`: small effect generators (bubbles, clods, steam, leaves/dirt, drips, grains, crust layers, whitecaps, splash, glints).
- `src/scenes.mjs`: 13 scenes (§3), plus `export const options = { gauge: { tone: "cold", icon: "drop" } }`.
- New kit assets in `kit/assets/` (§4.2), exported from `kit/assets/index.mjs`.
- Kit/component changes in §5.2 and `build.mjs` changes in §5.3. List all of them in `KIT_CHANGES.md`.

## 3. Scenes by beat

### 3.0 World model (shared by all scenes)

**Units.** World units (wu). The camera maps a world point to screen px. A framing `(fx, fy, s)` puts world point `(fx, fy)` at screen `(540, 900)`. The transform string is `translate(${540 - s*fx} ${900 - s*fy}) scale(${s})`, rounded to 2 decimals, always with all three numbers so GSAP can interpolate it. Use `toScreen(cam, x, y) = [540 + (x - fx)*s, 900 + (y - fy)*s]` for anchors.

**Water line (the backbone).** `L(x) = 740 + 0.064 * (x - 190)` is the water surface of the channel, falling steadily from left to right (exaggerated: 3.7°). It never rises except inside the siphon pipe. Reference values:
L(205)=741.0 · L(545)=762.7 · L(560)=763.7 · L(870)=783.5 · L(900)=785.4 · L(1120)=799.5 · L(1340)=813.6 · L(1370)=815.5 · L(1725)=838.2 · L(1748)=839.7 · L(1830)=845.0 · L(1920)=850.7 · L(1955)=853.0.

**Safe-zone rules used for every anchor:**
- Callout cards are 560 px wide, so `card.x ≤ 380`.
- Cards sit either in the **top band** `y = 590` (bottom ≤ 758 even at the 168 px two-line height) with every scene subject below screen y 760, or in the **bottom band** `y ≤ 1050` (bottom ≤ 1218) with the subject above it.

- Callouts and labels appear only while the camera is static. Use anchor `at`/`until` (§5.3) so a callout disappears before any camera move.

## 6. Timing rules
- **Source of truth.** `build.mjs` resolves each script cue word to the start of its timed word in `timings.<lang>.json` (normalised, Nth occurrence). Scenes use only `ctx.cue(action)`, `ctx.beat(id).start/end`, `ctx.t0/t1` and fixed offsets from them. Do not use `ctx.word()` with literal words (they break other languages), and type no absolute seconds.
- **Minimum durations:**
  - entrance pop/fade 0.2–0.3 s
  - element rise 0.4–0.6 s
  - exit fade 0.2–0.3 s
  - camera move ≥ 0.8 s (normal 1.2–1.4 s)
  - ghost on-screen 0.8–1.5 s, plus a 0.3 s fade-out ("rewind")
  - callout on-screen ≥ 1.2 s (warn below)
- **Fitting when a beat is short.** Use one helper: `fit(at, want, limit, min) = max(min, min(want, limit - at - 0.05))`, where `limit` = the next cue in the scene or `ctx.t1`. Then:
  - Ghost windows end before the next fix cue: `ghostDur = fit(cue, 1.3, nextCue, 0.8)`.
  - Chained offsets (e.g. `+0.8`, `+1.4`) are scaled by `k = min(1, (limit - cue)/plannedChain)`.
  - Loops compute their finite repeat count from the available span.
  - If an animation still cannot fit at its minimum, start it at the previous cue (never later than its own cue) and log a builder note.
- **Nothing is scheduled after `ctx.t1 - 0.05`.** Scene clips cut at the next scene start. Persistent elements are re-drawn in the next scene in their built state, so cuts are seamless.
- **Overlay windows.** Overlays end at `hide` (≈ next beat − 0.1). Callouts and labels must not overlap a camera move in their scene (use `until`). Cue-driven overlay times in `en` are listed in §3.
- **Recap (b13)** has no words; its span is `ctx.t1 - ctx.t0` (2.8 s in `en`). Marker spacing = `min(0.24, (span - 0.6)/9)`.
